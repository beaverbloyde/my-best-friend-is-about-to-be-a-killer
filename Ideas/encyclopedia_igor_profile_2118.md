## The "Igor" Trans-Dimensional Case: Dual Biographies

The **"Igor" Trans-Dimensional Entanglement** refers to the extraordinary, recorded consciousness-translocation event resulting from the **Time Ripple of November 2117**, wherein two historically and dimensionally distinct logistics officers exchanged temporal coordinates across the multiverse membrane:

1. **Igor Valentinovich Romashin (b. 2082 – d. unknown, Dimension-01):** Senior Logistical Administrator for the Northern Yakut Agricultural-Transport Directorate of the RSFSR.
2. **Filipp Andreyevich Yegorov (b. 1898 – d. 2154, Origin: Dimension-Beta / Timeline-1921):** Quartermaster and Supply Officer of the 5th Red Army Detachment during the Russian Civil War (Trans-Baikal / Far Eastern Front).

The resulting displacement, legal investigation, and historical resolution in the winter of 2118 catalyzed the first empirical confirmation of the **Multiverse Membrane Hypothesis** in modern Soviet relativistic physics.

## 1. Biography: The 2118 Figure (Igor V. Romashin)

### 1.1 Early Career and Administrative Role
Born in Yakutsk in 2082 to a family of civil transit engineers, **Igor Valentinovich Romashin** graduated from the Khabarovsk Institute of Planned Economics in 2105. Known as a meticulous, if somewhat vain and eccentric, bureaucratic coordinator, Romashin was appointed to the Northern Logistics Directorate, managing heavy automated freight schedules between Tynda, Aldan, and the Pacific coastline.

### 1.2 The Luxury Vehicle Controversy
Romashin was a known automotive purist in an era dominated by public high-speed maglev and shared utility transport. He privately commissioned a bespoke, high-performance, manual-combustion turbine sports coupe (the *Russo-Balt 'Vector-2110'*). 

In October 2117, shortly before the Time Ripple, Romashin was already under preliminary administrative review by the State Control Commission for questionable allocations of provincial maintenance funds toward the specialized manufacture of replacement titanium pistons for his vehicle.

## 2. Biography: The Displaced Pioneer (Filipp A. Yegorov)

### 2.1 Revolutionary Origins and Red Army Service (1918–1921)
**Filipp Andreyevich Yegorov** was born in 1898 in the Vladimir Governorate. A railway mechanic's son, Yegorov joined the Bolshevik Party in 1918 and served as a Red Army quartermaster during the Russian Civil War. Stationed along the Trans-Baikal rail lines in the brutal winter of 1920–1921, Yegorov was responsible for grain requisitions, steam locomotive coal distribution, and ammunition transit under extreme wartime scarcity, famine, and sub-zero temperatures.

During the global electron displacement wave of November 2117, an unprecedented cross-membrane harmonic resonance occurred. Yegorov's consciousness was ripped from a freezing rail outpost near Chita in 1921 and swapped into the body of Igor Romashin in the Russian Far East of 2118.

## 3. The Three Months in Pogranichny (Dec 2117 – Feb 2118)

### 3.1 Administrative Adaptation during the Analog Blackout
Waking up in an alien world, Yegorov initially believed he had suffered a severe fever delirium. However, when the Time Ripple disabled automated civilian computing, street cameras, and facial recognition networks, Soviet society temporarily reverted to **manual paper manifests, physical signatures, and heavy diesel/steam rail logistics**—the precise environment Yegorov had mastered in 1921.

Assuming Romashin's identity, Yegorov spent 90 days efficiently routing grain, fuel, and heating supplies through the frozen mountain junctions, earning unprompted commendations from regional recovery committees.

### 3.2 The Automotive Repair Audit and Panic
Yegorov possessed extensive mechanical aptitude, having driven early 1920s French *Renault* armored cars and *Fiat* military staff vehicles. He managed to operate Romashin's manual turbine sports car. However, during a test drive in deep snow, he damaged the vehicle's hydraulic transmission.

Unfamiliar with modern 22nd-century decentralized public banking, Yegorov handled the repair billing using wartime requisition protocol, signing a state ledger voucher. This triggered an automated municipal anti-corruption flag.

### 3.3 The Decoy Death in the Mountain Pass
Conditioned by memories of Tsarist secret police (*Okhrana*) and early wartime summary executions, Yegorov panicked, believing he was about to be exposed and committed to an imperial psychiatric asylum (*zholty dom*).

Discovering the deceased body of an itinerant worker (Citizen Anatoly V. Ruzhin, who had died of natural cardiovascular arrest in a mountain snowstorm), Yegorov placed the body inside the sports car, ignited the fuel cell to destroy visual records, and fled on foot into the Pogranichny border forests.

## 4. The "Anonymous Breadcrumbs" & The Investigative Climax

### 4.1 The Myth of the "Phantom Serial Killer"
Yegorov intended to flee across the border into China. However, upon hearing local railway gossip that a young PIRMGD researcher (**Luka Huo**) had correctly identified the burnt decoy corpse as an unrelated citizen using spacetime perception, Yegorov realized that Huo was the only human being in 2118 capable of seeing the truth without branding him a lunatic.

Yegorov remained in Pogranichny, shadowing Luka Huo and leaving cryptic physical clues:
* Notes folded in distinct Red Army triangular field creases (*treugolniki*).
* Pre-1918 orthographic annotations in purple indelible pencil.
* Strategic tip-offs (such as pointing out overlooked evidence at crime scenes), which initially led the Pogranichny ROVD to suspect a predatory stalker before the benevolent nature of the informant was realized.

### 4.2 The Forward-Projection Epiphany & Multiverse Proof
Following the resolution of the PIRMGD Containment Crisis on February 22, 2118, Luka Huo and Junior Lieutenant Nadezhda Morozova tracked Yegorov to a decommissioned railway boiler shed near Station Grodekovo.

Through a rigorous process of elimination, Huo discarded the initial hypothesis of a historical reenactor. When Huo engaged his forward-projecting spacetime perception upon Yegorov, he perceived a future worldline branch depicting Yegorov standing amidst the cobblestones and steam engines of a **1920s metropolis**.

Because Huo’s cognitive faculty operated **strictly forward along the time axis**, observing an authentic 1920s environment proved with absolute mathematical certainty that Yegorov did not originate from the past of Dimension-01, but from an **active parallel timeline offset within the Multiverse**.

Upon hearing Huo address him by his true 1921 name and offer unconditional legal advocacy, Yegorov collapsed to his knees in profound emotional catharsis, mourning that he was the sole pioneer to witness the peaceful future his generation had bled to construct.

## 5. Post-Crisis Settlement and Legacy

### 5.1 Legal Amnesty and Social Integration
In March 2118, the Supreme Soviet of the USSR, upon receiving a joint petition from FEBRAS and the MVD Directorate, issued a Special Humanitarian Decree granting Filipp A. Yegorov full legal citizenship, veteran status, and total immunity from the previous corruption inquest against Romashin's estate.

### 5.2 Historical Research Contributions
Yegorov was appointed Senior Historical and Logistical Consultant to the Far Eastern Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences. Over the next two decades, his firsthand eyewitness testimonies, oral histories of the Civil War period, and comparative socio-linguistic analyses provided Soviet historians with unprecedented insights into the early revolutionary era.

### 5.3 Later Life in 22nd-Century Pogranichny
Filipp Yegorov chose to remain permanently in Pogranichny, close to the railway yards and the PIRMGD campus. He became a beloved community figure, known for playing classical revolutionary songs on the seven-string guitar at the Municipal Youth Palace and maintaining a close, lifelong friendship with Luka Huo, Nadezhda Morozova, and Aleksey Volkov.

Yegorov passed away peacefully in his home in Pogranichny in the spring of 2154 at the age of 156 (biological age: 62), surrounded by friends and three generations of students. He is buried in the Pogranichny Memorial Cemetery under the title:  
**"A Red Army Soldier of 1921 — An Honored Citizen of the 22nd Century."**
