# Pogranichny 2118: Character Cast & Full Names Reference

This document serves as the master naming, role, and relational quick-reference sheet for the novel and game.

---

## 1. Primary Protagonists

### **Luka Huo** | *Лука Хо* | *霍卢卡 (Huò Lúkǎ)*
* **Full Legal Name:** Luka Huo *(voluntary omission of patronymic under Sino-Soviet mixed-heritage civic guidelines)*
* **Mother's Proposed Patronymic:** Anovich *(from father An Huo — creates the inside joke "Luka A. Huo" / "A-hole")*
* **Nicknames / Diminutives:** A-Huo (阿霍), Comrade Huo.
* **Role / Title:** Associate Spacetime Researcher, Theoretical Department at PIRM.
* **Background:** Half-Russian, half-Chinese. Born to HIT materials engineer father (An Huo) and Russian geophysicist mother (Elena Huo). Speaks fluent Russian and Dongbeihua.
* **Ability:** Passive forward-projecting 4D worldline observation ($\Delta t > 0$).

---

### **Junior Lieutenant Nadezhda Semyonovna Morozova** | *Младший лейтенант Надежда Семёновна Морозова*
* **Short / Intimate Forms:** Nadya (Надя), Nadyusha (Надюша).
* **Role / Title:** Junior Lieutenant of Militia (*Mladshiy Leytenant Militsii*), Duty Desk Officer at Pogranichny District ROVD (later promoted to Senior Lieutenant / *Starshiy Leytenant*).
* **Education:** Graduate of the Vladivostok Higher School of the MVD.
* **Dynamic:** Sharp proceduralist, energetic, deeply moral. Platonic buddy-cop partner to Luka; romantic crush on reporter Lin Chen.

---

### **Aleksey Dmitrievich Volkov** | *Алексей Дмитриевич Волков*
* **Short / Intimate Forms:** Lyosha (Лёша), Alyosha (Алёша), Alex (Алекс).
* **Role / Title:** Applied Experimental Physicist & Hardware Engineer at PIRM.
* **Dynamic:** Luka’s closest friend, hardware collaborator, and first guide to Russian culture/slang upon arriving from Harbin. The fated subject of Luka’s three-month recurring nightmare.

---

## 2. Investigation & Press Ensemble

### **Major Viktor Danilovich Tarasov** | *Майор юстиции Виктор Данилович Тарасов*
* **Role / Title:** Major of Justice (*Mayor Yustitsii*), Senior Investigator of Criminal Inquiries (*Starshiy Sledovatel*) at Pogranichny District ROVD.
* **Personality:** Stoic, iron-faced procedural master; secret fanatic of Golden Age classic detective novels. Mentors Nadya with stern, demanding discipline.

---

### **Lin Chen** | *Линь Чэнь* | *林晨 (Lín Chén)*
* **Role / Title:** Border Bureau Correspondent for the *Heilongjiang People's Daily* / Joint Xinhua-TASS Cross-Border News Service (Pogranichny–Suifenhe Bureau).
* **Personality / Dynamic:** Confident, bilingual (Russian / Dongbei dialect), sharp-witted investigative reporter. Pre-existing press acquaintance of Luka; romantic interest for Nadya Morozova.

---

### **Dr. Kira Romanovna Vlasova** | *Доктор Кира Романовна Власова*
* **Role / Title:** Chief Forensic Medical Examiner (*Sudmedekspert*), Pogranichny ROVD / District Hospital.
* **Personality:** Pragmatic, razor-tongued, veteran pathologist completely unbothered by "supernatural" claims; relies strictly on physical anatomy and chemistry.

---

## 3. Pogranichny District ROVD Precinct Personnel

### **Colonel Pavel Alexeyevich Zaytsev** | *Полковник Павел Алексеевич Зайцев*
* **Role / Title:** Chief of the Pogranichny ROVD (*Nachal'nik ROVD*).
* **Profile:** Weary executive administrator managing district heating quotas, inter-bureau politics, and Vladivostok teletypes.

---

### **Senior Sergeant Semyon Timofeyevich Korobov** | *Старший сержант Семён Тимофеевич Коробов*
* **Common Nickname:** "Uncle Senya" (*Дядя Сеня*).
* **Role / Title:** Senior Desk Sergeant (*Starshiy Dezhurnyy Serzhant*), Pogranichny District ROVD.
* **Rank & Career Background:** A 25+ year veteran of the district militsiya. Famously turned down promotions to Warrant Officer (*Praporshchik*) and the quartermaster track decades ago to avoid the nightmare of "material liability" (*materialnaya otvetstvennost'*) and Gosplan inventory audits, as well as refusing mandatory reassignment/schooling in Vladivostok. 
* **Precinct Dynamic:** The beloved, unflappable "front desk soul" and fatherly anchor of the station. Perpetual brewer of herbal taiga tea and cracker of cedar nuts. While technically outranked by young academy-graduate officers like Junior Lieutenant Nadya Morozova, he is treated with deep respect and fond deference by the entire precinct; he affectionately covers Nadya's counter duties so she can pursue field investigations.

---

### **Captain Boris Mikhailovich Lavrov** | *Капитан Борис Михайлович Лавров*
* **Short Form:** Borya (*Боря*).
* **Role / Title:** Senior Operative of Criminal Search (*Starshiy Oper*). Field-smart detective in a flight jacket, Tarasov's pragmatic right hand.

---

### **Yevgeny "Zhenya" Belov** | *Евгений (Женя) Белов*
* **Role / Title:** Balanced-Ternary Mainframe Custodian & Archive Specialist (*Setun-4M* Terminal Operator) at Pogranichny ROVD.

---

### **Junior Sergeant Yuri / Yulia Sergeyevich / Sergeyevna Sorokin** | *Младший сержант Юрий / Юлия Сергеевич / Сергеевна Сорокин / Сорокина*
* **Role / Title:** Junior Patrol Officer at Pogranichny ROVD.
* **Narrative Arc:** Subject of Luka's "Innate Truth" premonition (transitioning from Yuri to Yulia).

## 4. Key Case Figures & Antagonists

### **Filipp Antonovich Yegorov ("Alt-Igor")** | *Филипп Антонович Егоров*
* **Original Identity:** 1921 Red Army Quartermaster / Logistics Officer from an alternate Civil War timeline.
* **Current Status:** Consciousness swapped across the Multiverse during the Time Ripple into the 2118 body of Igor Romashin. Staged his own death to escape what he feared was modern Tsarist/Cheka retaliation.

---

### **Igor Valentinovich Romashin (The Real 2118 Igor)** | *Игорь Валентинович Ромашин*
* **Role / Title:** High-ranking Provincial Logistics Director, Gosplan Far East Freight Administration.
* **Current Status:** Trapped back in 1921 Civil War Russia.

---

### **Anatoly Viktorovich Ruzhin** | *Анатолий Викторович Ружин*
* **Role:** The deceased man in the charred sports car (Case 1).
* **Cause of Death:** Natural cardiovascular failure caused by severe post-Ripple cold and alcohol consumption.

---

### **Valery Pavlovich Kuzmin** | *Валерий Павлович Кузьмин*
* **Short Form:** Valera (*Валера*).
* **Role:** Anatoly Ruzhin’s drinking companion. Latent uncataloged Chrono-Sensitive who unknowingly triggered a 4D spatial fold (teleportation) across the Nesterovka River.

---

### **Dr. Nikolai Grigoryevich Krotov** | *Доктор Николай Григорьевич Кротов*
* **Role / Title:** Senior Physicist / Department Head at PIRM.
* **Narrative Role:** The rogue researcher who secretly compartmentalized the extraction and containment of the unstable Time Anomaly in Sub-Level 3.
