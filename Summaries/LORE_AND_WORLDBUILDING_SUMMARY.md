# Worldbuilding & Lore Reference Summary

A comprehensive synthesis of the historical, scientific, technological, and institutional lore of **Pogranichny 2118**.

---

## 🌍 1. Geopolitical & Historical Setting

* **Date & Location**: Winter, **April 2118 AD**, **Pogranichny District**, Primorsky Krai, Russian SFR, USSR.
* **The Society**: The Soviet Union never collapsed; mid-20th-century cybernetic planning and structural reforms achieved **universal basic needs worldwide by 2050**.
* **Sino-Soviet Border Integration**: Deep cultural and scientific integration across the Far Eastern corridor connecting Vladivostok, Pogranichny, and Harbin (bilingual academies, shared research fellowships, high-speed rail lines).
* **Social Norms**: Full legal equality, recognition of matronymics (*Matchestvo*), non-binary naming flexibility, and voluntary omission of patronymics for mixed-heritage citizens. Violent crime and homicide are extreme statistical anomalies.

## 🌌 2. The Time Ripple & Spacetime Mechanics

### The Catastrophe (3 Months Prior)
* A cosmic wave swept through spacetime, causing localized temporal disturbances, pocket distortions (acceleration, deceleration, temporal pauses), and widespread **electron displacement**.
* **Ripple Sickness (*Ryab'evaya bolezn'*)**: Kinetic-temporal desynchronization of the central nervous system. Unlike radiation, it is non-ionizing and structurally leaves DNA undamaged, but induces sensory lag, spatial vertigo, and synaptic fatigue.

### Chrono-Sensitives (The 1 in 300,000)
* Cognitive and neurological sensitivity (not physical or genetic mutation). Synaptic pathways synchronized with background temporal frequencies, expanding conscious perception beyond linear time.
* **The 4D Paper Strip Model**: Just as a 2D sheet bends through 3D space to touch its ends, 3D space folds using the **4th Dimension (Time)** as the bending hinge.
* **Luka's Premonition Mechanism**:
  * An observational beam projected forward along the target's worldline ribbon.
  * **Strict Forward-Only Law**: Operates strictly forward ($\Delta t > 0$); cannot look backward into the past.
  * **Snapping to High-Entropy States**: Luka cannot choose exact timestamps; perception naturally snaps to the most informationally dense, transformative future state of that matter (e.g. an inscribed gravestone, a promotion banner, or a crisis).
* **Quantum Non-Determinism (Fluid Futures)**:
  * The timeline is a braided manifold of quantum superpositions. Premonitions reveal the **highest-probability worldline branch** based on current present variables.
  * Introducing new information, conscious choices, or physical interventions in the present collapses the wave-function onto a new strand, altering the future outcome.

## 💻 3. Technology & The "Analog Forensic Shift"

### Balanced-Ternary Computing (*Setun* Legacy)
* Computing infrastructure runs on **balanced ternary logic gates** $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ (*False, Indeterminate/Unknown, True*).
* Extremely efficient at neural modeling, machine learning, and macro-logistics, but flags anomalous spacetime events as `INVALID NOISE / STATE 0 (INDETERMINATE)`.

### The Detective's Sensor Void
* Unshielded civilian mobile networks, digital cameras, automated facial recognition, and digital spectrometers were fried during the Time Ripple.
* **The Return to Analog**: Police and forensic work relies on **physical footprints, ballistics, chemical reagents, eyewitness interviews, and dual-copy paper manifests**.
* **E-Paper Displays**: Flexible electro-cellulose sheets using magnetic micro-pigments that consume power only during refresh cycles, co-existing with traditional ballpoint pen paper copies for post-Ripple audit compliance.

### Municipal Infrastructure
* **Teploset (Теплосеть)**: District steam heating grid piping superheated steam from central plants to keep sub-zero Siberian winters (-25°C to -35°C) survivable.
* **Aerogel Insulation**: Ultralight synthetic porous solids with minimal thermal conductivity used in modernized Soviet neoclassical architecture.

## 🏢 4. Key Institutions & Terminology

### PIRMGD / PIRM (ПИРМГД / ПИРМ)
* **Full Name**: Pogranichny Institute of Relativistic Mechanics and Geo-Cosmic Dynamics (*Пограничный Институт Релятивистской Механики и Геокосмической Динамики*).
* **Role**: Premier Soviet Far Eastern research institution studying relativistic physics, anomaly metrics, and spacetime mechanics.

### Pogranichny District ROVD (РОВД)
* **Full Name**: Pogranichny District Department of Internal Affairs (*Районный отдел внутренних дел*), operating under the MVD (*Militsiya*).
* **Staffing**: ~50–60 sworn personnel serving a district of ~35,000–52,000 citizens.
* **Forms of Address**:
  * *Tovarishch* (Comrade): Used for colleagues, equals, and law-abiding citizens.
  * *Grazhdanin* (Citizen): Formally used by law enforcement when addressing suspects or detainees.
  * *Podpiska o nevyezde* (Подписка о невыезде): Official written recognizance pledge barring a suspect from leaving the municipal district.
