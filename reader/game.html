Welcome to the LOGOS-3 Incident Reconstruction Terminal. As an investigative analyst, your objective is to review timeline fragments, decrypt intelligence dossiers, collect vital keywords, and complete the official Case Docket.
01 🔍 Collect Keywords

Hover over or tap highlighted [Keywords] in observations, smart devices, audio logs, and dossiers to add them to your Evidence Tray.

02 🗂️ Examine Clues & Lore

Navigate the Timeline bar to inspect scenes chronologically. Open the Lore Codex tab to decrypt background personnel files and drag open multi-window dossiers.

03 📋 Assemble the Docket

Press Space or click Open Case Docket. Drag words from your tray (or click a word then click a blank) into [ ? ] slots, and type numerical deductions.

04 ⚖️ Verify Your Theory

Click Verify Deduction. The system evaluates your theory with three status levels: ✓ Correct, ⚠️ Almost there (<3 errors), or ❌ Incorrect.

💡 PRO INVESTIGATOR TIPS:
  • Windows (Docket and Lore dossiers) are draggable and can be arranged anywhere on your desk. Clicking any window brings it to the front.
  • You can drag words directly from one docket slot to another, or drag them back to the tray to clear them.
  • Look out for environmental details: calendars, clocks, audio logs, and prescription stamps often hold crucial deductive math!
